Tier 03: Mosque & Waqf Governance

إِدَارَةُ الْمَسَاجِدِ وَالأَوْقَافِ
Rule 3.1 • Waqf Act Sec 28

First Charge Rule (Repairs)

Repair and physical maintenance of Waqf property is the strict first charge on all income before charitable distribution.

Order: 1. Repairs ➔ 2. Objects ➔ 3. Poor
Rule 3.2 • Sec 20 & 39

Prohibition on Property Sale

Mutawallis cannot sell, mortgage, or execute long-term leases on Waqf assets without prior Supreme Court or Waqf Board sanction.

Unlawful Alienation = Breach of Trust
Rule 3.3 • Waqf Act Sec 44

Annual Audited Accounts

Mutawallis must prepare accounts by March 1st and file audited copies with the Board of Waqf Commissioners by April 1st annually.

Non-compliance = Fines & Removal Risk
Rule 3.4 • FCC & Waqf Act

Dual-Custody Cash Box Protocol

Prohibiting single-person cash collection to comply with Financial Crimes Commission (FCC) anti-money laundering standards.

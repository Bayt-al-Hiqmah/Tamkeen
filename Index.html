Muslim Family Council (MFC) & Nikah Registration

Muslim Marriage (Nikah) & Mauritian Civil Status

Under Mauritian administrative regulations, the Muslim Family Council (MFC) oversees the formal registration of Islamic marriages (Nikah). Balancing religious validity with full statutory civil protection.

  • Dual Registration: Ensuring Islamic Nikah is formally recognized under civil law to safeguard spousal and child rights.
  • Family Mediation: MFC tools for resolving matrimonial disputes before court litigation.

Society, Mosque & Waqf Governance

إِدَارَةُ الْمَسَاجِدِ وَالأَوْقَافِ
Rule 3.1 • Waqf Act Sec 28

First Charge Rule (Repairs)

Under Section 28 of the Waqf Act, repair and physical maintenance of Waqf property is the strict first charge on all income before any charitable distribution.

Order of Funds: 1. Repairs ➔ 2. Objects ➔ 3. Poor
Rule 3.2 • Waqf Act Sec 20 & 39

Prohibition on Property Sale

Mutawallis cannot sell, mortgage, or execute long-term leases on Waqf assets without prior Supreme Court or Waqf Board permission.

Unlawful Alienation = Breach of Trust
Rule 3.3 • Waqf Act Sec 44

Annual Audited Accounts

Mutawallis must prepare accounts by March 1st and file audited copies to the Board of Waqf Commissioners by April 1st annually.

Non-compliance = Fines & Prosecution
Rule 3.4 • FCC & Waqf Act

Dual-Custody Cash Box Protocol

Prohibiting single-person cash collection to satisfy Financial Crimes Commission (FCC) anti-money laundering regulations.

Key Islamic Institutions & Pluralism in Mauritius

Balancing Sectoral Harmony, Theological Diversity & Secular Oversight

Statutory & Communal Landscape
Board of Waqf Commissioners

Established under Waqf Act 1941. Oversees all Muslim trusts, maintains waqfnama records, and enforces financial compliance.

Islamic Cultural Centre (ICC)

Parliamentary statutory body handling cultural affairs, national Islamic heritage, and Hajj pilgrimage logistics.

Jamiat-Ul-Ulama Mauritius (JUM)

Council of Deobandi scholars coordinating religious rulings (Fatwas), mediation, and educational curriculums.

World Islamic Mission (WIM) & SIM

WIM represents Barelwi traditionalist institutions. Société Islamique de Maurice (SIM, est. 1971) focuses on youth outreach and literature.
